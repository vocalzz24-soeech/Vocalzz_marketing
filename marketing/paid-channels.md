# Paid marketing channels for Vocalzz: cost and conversion guide (Nov 2026)

Figures are **India, healthcare/therapy, Nov 2026**. Where we have **our own data** it is marked "ours"; everything
else is a range from agency/vendor benchmarks and experience (directional, not guaranteed). Replace with real numbers
after each test month.

**Definitions:** *CPC* = cost per click. *CPM* = cost per 1,000 views. *Lead* = a real parent who calls or WhatsApps.
*Click→lead* = share of clicks that become a lead. A lead then still has to book an assessment (typically 30–50%) and
start therapy (typically 40–60% of assessments).

Fit: ★★★★★ = start here, ★ = skip for now.

## What to use first (₹10k a month)
1. **Google Search ads**: about ₹6,000 (highest intent; proven cheap for speech terms).
2. **Meta Click-to-WhatsApp** (Instagram + Facebook): about ₹4,000 (Hinglish parent-worry reels).
3. **Free, alongside:** Google Business Profile posts and reviews, WhatsApp messages to old enquiries, one society
   screening camp a month, paediatrician/preschool referrals.
4. **Later (month 3+):** retargeting, Practo Prime for audiology/adults, WhatsApp Status ads once pricing is clearer.
5. **Skip:** display banners, Sulekha/UrbanPro, hoardings, LinkedIn.
