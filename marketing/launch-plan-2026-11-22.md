## Meta Click-to-WhatsApp campaign

**Before:** 99106 71188 connected to Instagram and the Facebook page (checklist #2).

**Ads Manager → Create**
- Objective: **Engagement** → conversion location **Messaging apps → WhatsApp** (number 99106 71188) →
  performance goal **Maximise number of conversations**.
- Budget: **₹70/day**, start Sunday 9 am, no end date (pause manually).
- Location: drop a pin on Logix Blossom County, **radius 12 km**. Age **24–45**, all genders.
- Detailed targeting: **none** (Meta no longer allows targeting by health conditions; let it find parents).
- Placements: **Advantage+** (Instagram + Facebook Reels, Stories, Feeds).
- Message template (what parents see when the chat opens): greeting "Namaste! Please share your child's age
  and your main concern, and we'll guide you." Quick questions: *My child isn't talking yet* · *Stammering* ·
  *Book an assessment* · *Fees and timings*.
- 3 ads, one per reel below. Same primary text pattern, one problem each:
  - Reel 1 text: "2 saal ka bachha abhi bhi nahi bolta? Har bachha alag hota hai, par kuch signs pe dhyaan dena
    zaroori hai. Sector 137, Noida mein speech therapy. WhatsApp karein, assessment book karein."
  - Reel 2 text: "Bolte waqt atak jaate hain? Stammering ke liye practical techniques, teens aur adults ke liye.
    Sector 137, Noida. WhatsApp karein."
  - Reel 3 text: "Har session ke baad parents ko milta hai ghar par practice ka plan. Speech, OT aur behaviour
    therapy ek hi jagah. Sector 137, Noida. WhatsApp karein."

**Reel scripts** (vertical video, 15–25 seconds, Dr. Tanu on camera, Hinglish, subtitles on). Start with the face
and the worry in the first 2 seconds (71% of viewers skip in the first 3 seconds).
1. **Late talker**: "Aapka 2 saal ka bachha abhi bhi 'mumma-papa' nahi bolta?" → "Har bachha alag hota hai, par
   in 3 signs pe dhyaan dijiye: naam pe response na dena, ishaara na karna, 2 saal tak 2-word phrases na bolna." →
   "Main Dr. Tanu Garg, 10+ saal se bachchon ke saath kaam kar rahi hoon." → "WhatsApp karein, assessment book karein."
2. **Stammering**: "Interview ya class mein bolte waqt atak jaate ho?" → "Stammering mein practical techniques aur
   roz ki practice madad kar sakti hai." → "Teens aur adults ke liye sessions, Sector 137 Noida." → "WhatsApp karein."
3. **Inside a session** (only with written parent consent; child's face not shown): therapist and child playing →
   voiceover "Therapy bachche ke liye khel jaisi, aur har session ke baad parents ko ghar ka practice plan." →
   "Speech, OT aur behaviour therapy, ek hi jagah." → "WhatsApp karein."

No cure, timeline or "best" claims. "Every child is different" stays in.
