# Reel scripts: first ad set (Nov 2026)

Three reels for the Instagram/Facebook Click-to-WhatsApp ads starting Sunday 22 Nov, also posted on @vocalzz_4u.
Each is **20–25 seconds, vertical 9:16, Hinglish, subtitles on**. Speaker: **Dr. Tanu Garg** (unless noted).

**Before shooting:** Dr. Tanu to check the milestone wording in Reel 1 and the technique wording in Reel 2.

---

## Shooting setup (all three, one 45-minute session)
- **Where:** the consultation desk with the lit VOCALZZ sign behind (it's on brand and instantly recognisable).
  Dr. Tanu sits facing a window or a bright light; no light directly behind her head.
- **Camera:** back camera of the phone, 1080p 30 fps, HDR off, lens wiped. Phone on a stand at **eye level**,
  about an arm and a half away. Frame from mid-chest up, with space above the head.
- **Sound:** clip-on mic on the collar; fan and AC off while recording.
- **Clothes:** plain solid colour (blue, maroon, bottle green or white); avoid small checks or stripes (they shimmer).
- **How to record:** one line at a time, **3 takes each**, pause 2 seconds before and after each line. Easier to
  edit, and no need to memorise the whole script.
- **Energy:** warm, like talking to one worried parent across the desk; smile at the start and at the end.
- **Children:** only with **written parent consent**; otherwise hands-only shots or faces turned away/blurred.

## Editing (CapCut or Instagram editor, ~20 min per reel)
1. Put the best take of each line in order; trim the pauses so it moves quickly.
2. **Auto captions** on (Hinglish/Hindi), then fix spellings by hand. White text, black outline or box, middle of
   the screen (keep the bottom third clear for Instagram's caption and button).
3. The **hook text** (first row of each table) on screen from the very first frame, large, top third.
4. Background music very low (or none) so the voice is clear.
5. End card (2 s): Vocalzz logo + "WhatsApp 99106 71188 · Sector 137, Noida".
6. Export **1080p, 30 fps**. Post on Wi-Fi with "Upload at highest quality" on.
