# Organic growth plan (SEO + Google listing): Dec 2026 – Mar 2027

**Goal:** by March 2027, enough parents find Vocalzz on Google *without ads* that the ad budget can come down
step by step. Not to zero overnight: we cut ads only when the organic numbers show they can carry the load.

**Honest expectations**
- **Google Business Profile (the map listing)** moves fastest: weeks. It is already the main lead source
  (~11 calls, ~85 direction requests a month, Aug–Oct 2026), so it gets the most effort.
- **Website pages and blog posts** take **3–6 months** to rank. Work done in December pays off from about March.
- Nobody can guarantee rankings. What we control: a complete listing, lots of genuine reviews, useful pages, steady
  posting, and local links.

## The plan in 5 parts

### 1. Google listing: the biggest lever (owner, ~30 min/week)
- **Reviews, every week.** Ask every family after a good session. Use the review link / QR at reception and in the
  WhatsApp "thank you" message. Target: **+4 to 6 genuine reviews a month** (never paid, never written by staff,
  no discounts in exchange). Reply to **every** review within 2 days (Claude drafts).
- **One post a week** (Claude writes): a parent tip, a team intro, a therapy-room photo. Same topics as the reels.
- **2–3 new photos a week**: clinic outside (helps people find the door), rooms, team. Children only with written
  consent, otherwise faces hidden.
- **Services list complete** with short descriptions (speech, OT, special ed, audiology, physio, home sessions, online).
- **Q&A:** add 5 common questions and answers (fees process, first visit, home visits, online therapy, parking).
- **Categories:** check the primary and secondary categories match what you offer (Claude checks with a screenshot).
- **Fix duplicates** after the phone appeal: merge/remove "Audiology centre" and the "Rehabilitation clinic"
  listing so all reviews and calls land on one profile.
- **Rename** to "Vocalzz Speech & Audio" once the phone change is approved.

### 2. Website foundations (Claude, with 15 min from owner)
- **Set up Google Search Console** (domain vocalzz.com, auto-verified through Cloudflare), submit the sitemap.
  Then connect it to Windsor so Claude can read which searches show the site each month.
- Bing Webmaster Tools (import from Search Console, 5 min).
- Add the **opening date / "since July 2022"** and team details to the clinic schema; link every new page from the
  services menu and related pages.
- Show 3–4 real Google reviews (text, with first name only, with permission) on the home page.

### 4. Blog: 2 posts a month (Claude drafts, Dr. Tanu checks)
Each post answers one parent worry, ends with "every child is different" and the WhatsApp button. First 8:
1. My 2-year-old isn't talking: when to wait and when to get an assessment
2. Speech delay vs autism: what parents notice, and why an assessment helps
3. 10 speech therapy mistakes parents make (the best-performing Instagram topic)
4. Screen time and speech: what research says, simple home swaps
5. Stammering in children: what to do and not do at home
6. Signs of hearing loss in babies and toddlers
7. Handwriting and pencil grip: when OT can help
8. Hindi/English at home: does bilingual slow speech? (Hinglish-friendly topic)
Reels on the same topics get embedded in the post (YouTube Shorts too: they appear in Google results).

### 5. Local links and listings (owner + Claude, ~1 hour/month)
- **Same name, address, number everywhere** (Justdial, Practo, Sulekha, Lybrate, Bing Places, Apple Maps,
  Facebook, Instagram): "Vocalzz Speech & Audio, Tower E-004, Logix Blossom County, Sector 137, Noida 201304,
  +91 99106 71188". Claude makes a copy-paste sheet.
- **Nearby preschools and schools** (e.g. the EuroKids and Footprints branches near Sector 141): offer a free
  parent talk on speech milestones; ask for a mention/link on their parent resources page.
- **Paediatricians and ENT doctors** within 5 km: a one-page referral note; listing on their "referral partners".
- **Society RWAs** (Logix Blossom County and neighbouring towers): parent-talk or newsletter mention.
- Local news / parenting sites: one expert quote or article a quarter from Dr. Tanu.

## How we decide to cut ad spend
Count new enquiries by source every month ("How did you hear about us?": Claude can add this to the booking app).
- If **organic enquiries (Google listing + website + referrals) ≥ ad enquiries for 2 months running**, cut the ad
  budget by a third and watch for a month.
- Keep ads on the keywords that bring patients and that we don't yet rank for (often "near me" searches).
- The ₹20,000 credit runs to 21 Jan 2027 at ₹350/day, so no cuts before then anyway.
