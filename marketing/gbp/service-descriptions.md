## Speech & Hearing Specialist

**Speech therapy** (265 chars)

```
Speech and language therapy for children and adults at our Sector 137, Noida clinic: late talking, unclear speech, autism, stammering, voice and stroke-related difficulties. We start with an assessment, set goals with you and give home practice after every session.
```

**Child Speech Therapy** (241 chars)

```
Speech therapy for children who are late to talk, hard to understand or find it difficult to use words and sentences. Play-based sessions in Sector 137, Noida, with simple activities for parents to practise at home. Every child is different.
```

**Speech Therapy for Autism** (249 chars)

```
Communication support for autistic children: understanding, gestures, words, play and social interaction. Sessions are planned around your child's strengths, and parents learn ways to support communication at home. WhatsApp us to book an assessment.
```

**Child Behavioral Therapy** (233 chars)

```
Support for children with tantrums, attention or behaviour concerns, planned together with our speech and occupational therapists. Parents get practical strategies to use at home. We start with an assessment to understand your child.
```

**Stammering Treatment** (250 chars)

```
Stammering (हकलाना) therapy for children, teens and adults in Sector 137, Noida and online. Practical techniques for easier speech, real-life practice for class, calls and interviews, and parent guidance for young children. Every person is different.
```

**Late Talker / Speech Delay Therapy** (238 chars)

```
For toddlers saying fewer words than expected, not joining words, or not talking yet. An assessment shows where your child is, and you get simple activities for meals, bath and play. Getting advice early does no harm. WhatsApp us to book.
```

**Adult Speech Therapy** (216 chars)

```
Speech therapy for adults: stammering at work, voice problems, and speech, language or swallowing difficulties after a stroke or illness. Sessions at our Sector 137, Noida clinic or online, planned around your goals.
```

**Voice Therapy** (219 chars)

```
For a hoarse, tired or weak voice, common in teachers, singers and people who talk a lot at work. Healthy voice use and exercises guided by a speech therapist, at our Noida clinic or online. We start with an assessment.
```

**Speech Therapy After Stroke (Aphasia)** (210 chars)

```
Help with understanding, finding words, speaking, reading and writing after a stroke. Family members learn how to support practice at home. Sessions at our Sector 137, Noida clinic, planned after an assessment.
```

**Swallowing Therapy** (220 chars)

```
Support for children and adults who find chewing or swallowing difficult, including after a stroke. Safer swallowing strategies and exercises, with guidance for family members. Assessment at our Sector 137, Noida clinic.
```

**Online Speech Therapy** (206 chars)

```
Live online speech therapy sessions for children and adults anywhere in India. Parents join children's sessions and get activities to practise between sessions. We'll advise whether online suits your needs.
```

**Speech Therapy at Home** (207 chars)

```
Speech therapy sessions at home in parts of Noida and Greater Noida, depending on therapist availability. Your child practises with their own toys and routines. WhatsApp us your sector to check availability.
```

## Audiologist

**Hearing Test** (216 chars)

```
Hearing tests for children and adults by an audiologist at our Sector 137, Noida clinic. We explain the results in simple words and suggest next steps. Clinic visits Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm, by appointment.
```

**Child Hearing Test** (215 chars)

```
Hearing checks for babies and children, for example with speech delay or if they don't respond to sounds or their name. Results explained clearly to parents, with speech therapy support in the same clinic if needed.
```

**Pediatric Audiologist** (180 chars)

```
Hearing assessment and guidance for children by an audiologist who is also a speech therapist, so hearing and speech are looked at together. Sector 137, Noida. WhatsApp us to book.
```

**Hearing Assessment** (160 chars)

```
A full hearing assessment for children and adults, with clear advice on what the results mean for daily life, school or work. Sector 137, Noida, by appointment.
```

**Hearing Aid Consultation** (194 chars)

```
Advice on whether a hearing aid may help, what to expect, and how to get used to it. If speech still feels hard to follow with a hearing aid, ask about speech therapy support in the same clinic.
```
