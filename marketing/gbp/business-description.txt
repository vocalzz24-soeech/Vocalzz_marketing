Vocalzz Speech & Audio is a multi-therapy clinic in Sector 137, Noida, for children, adults and seniors. Under one roof: speech therapy (late talkers, autism, stammering/हकलाना, unclear speech, voice, speech after a stroke), hearing tests and hearing aid consultation, occupational therapy, behavioural support and special education. Led by Dr. Tanu Garg, Speech-Language Therapist and Audiologist with 10+ years' experience (previously at Fortis Noida and BL Kapoor Hospital). Our team plans therapy together, and parents get home practice after every session. Clinic, online, or home sessions in parts of Noida and Greater Noida. Every child is different; we start with an assessment. Clinic visits Mon–Sat, 10 am–8 pm. WhatsApp us 7 days a week.
