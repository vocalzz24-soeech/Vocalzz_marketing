## Avoid
- Promising that hearing will "come back", be "cured" or be "100% clear"
- Claims about hearing aid brands, prices or "invisible" aids that the clinic hasn't confirmed
- Calling hearing aids "machines" in a way that sounds old or embarrassing. Use "hearing aid" or "sunne ki chhoti device".
- Scare tactics about dementia or isolation. You may say untreated hearing loss can affect daily life and connection with family, but keep it calm.
- Diagnosing in comments or DMs. Invite people to a hearing test instead.
- Mixing audiology with other services in one post (one problem per post)

## Safety lines (include when relevant)
- **Sudden hearing loss** in one or both ears, ear pain with fever, discharge from the ear, or dizziness with hearing loss: see an ENT doctor **urgently**, the same day. Never tell people to wait for a routine test.
- Don't advise ear cleaning with earbuds, oil or home remedies.
- Keep newborn and child hearing content general and encourage early checking; don't comment on a specific child's case online.
