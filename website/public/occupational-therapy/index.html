Our services

Occupational Therapy in Sector 137, Noida

Helping children build the everyday skills that make home and school easier, from holding a pencil to managing busy, noisy places.

Book an assessment Call +91 88002 30240

Who it can help

  • Children who are very sensitive to noise, touch, clothes or food, or who seek a lot of movement
  • Children who find buttons, scissors, cutlery or handwriting difficult
  • Children who find it hard to sit, focus or follow a routine
  • Children with autism, ADHD or developmental delay who need support with daily activities
  • Children who seem clumsy or find balance and coordination hard

What we work on

  • Sensory integration: helping the body process sensations calmly
  • Fine motor skills: grip, hand strength and control
  • Handwriting readiness and handwriting
  • Attention and organisation for learning
  • Daily living skills: dressing, eating and self-care

Working together with speech and behaviour therapy

Many children need more than one kind of support. Our occupational therapists, speech therapist and behaviour therapist plan together, so your child works towards one shared set of goals.

How it works at Vocalzz

  1. AssessmentWe meet you, listen to your concerns and understand strengths and needs.
  2. A plan made togetherClear goals, shared with you, and coordinated with other therapies if needed.
  3. Regular sessionsOne-on-one, in a group or online, depending on what suits best.
  4. Home practiceAfter every session you get simple things to practise at home.
  5. Progress reviewsWe review what's working and adjust the plan.

Every person is different, so we don't promise fixed results or timelines. We start with an assessment, set realistic goals with you and review progress regularly.

Frequently asked questions

What is sensory integration therapy?

It uses play-based activities to help children respond more comfortably to sights, sounds, touch and movement. Your therapist will explain how it may help your child after the assessment.

Is occupational therapy only for children with a diagnosis?

No. Many children without a diagnosis benefit from support with handwriting, coordination or daily routines.

What should my child wear?

Comfortable clothes they can move and play in.
