Who it can help

  • Children who are late talkers, have unclear speech, or show signs of autism or speech delay
  • Children who need help with sensory needs, fine motor skills, handwriting or attention (occupational therapy)
  • Teens and adults who stammer, and adults with speech or swallowing difficulties after a stroke

What we work on

  • Clinic visits in Sector 137, Noida: right next to the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway, easy to reach from Greater Noida
  • Home sessions of speech therapy and occupational therapy in some parts of Greater Noida, depending on therapist availability
  • Online speech therapy sessions from home, with practice activities you can do between sessions
  • A mix of the three: for example an assessment at the clinic, then some sessions at home or online

Getting to the clinic from Greater Noida

Our clinic is at Tower E-004, Logix Blossom County, Sector 137, Noida, next to the Noida–Greater Noida Expressway. Families from Greater Noida usually drive straight up the Expressway; travel time depends on where you start and on traffic, so plan a little extra time for the first visit. Clinic visits are Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm, by appointment, and we can suggest session times that avoid the busiest hours. Every child is different, so we start with an assessment and then plan sessions with you, at the clinic, at home or online.

How it works at Vocalzz

  1. AssessmentWe meet you, listen to your concerns and understand strengths and needs.
  2. A plan made togetherClear goals, shared with you, and coordinated with other therapies if needed.
  3. Regular sessionsOne-on-one, in a group or online, depending on what suits best.
  4. Home practiceAfter every session you get simple things to practise at home.
  5. Progress reviewsWe review what's working and adjust the plan.

Every person is different, so we don't promise fixed results or timelines. We start with an assessment, set realistic goals with you and review progress regularly.

Frequently asked questions

Do you visit homes in Greater Noida?

In some areas, yes, depending on therapist availability. WhatsApp us your sector or society and we'll tell you what's possible.

Is online speech therapy useful for my child?

For many children and adults it works well, especially when a parent joins and practises between sessions. For some needs an in-person assessment comes first. We'll suggest what fits after we understand your concern.

How do I book from Greater Noida?

WhatsApp us on +91 99106 71188 with your area, the age of the person and your main concern. We reply 7 days a week.
