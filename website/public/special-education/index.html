Our services

Special Education in Sector 137, Noida

One-on-one support for children who learn differently, so they can build skills and confidence at their own pace.

Book an assessment Call +91 88002 30240

Who it can help

  • Children who find reading, spelling or writing much harder than expected (including dyslexia)
  • Children who find numbers and maths concepts difficult
  • Children with attention or memory difficulties that affect learning
  • Children with autism or developmental delay who need adapted teaching
  • Children who need help keeping up with school work

What we work on

  • Reading, phonics and spelling
  • Writing and expressing ideas
  • Early numeracy and maths skills
  • Attention, memory and study skills
  • Individualised learning goals shared with parents and, where helpful, school

Learning support that fits with therapy

Our special educator works with our speech and occupational therapists, so learning goals and therapy goals support each other.

How it works at Vocalzz

  1. AssessmentWe meet you, listen to your concerns and understand strengths and needs.
  2. A plan made togetherClear goals, shared with you, and coordinated with other therapies if needed.
  3. Regular sessionsOne-on-one, in a group or online, depending on what suits best.
  4. Home practiceAfter every session you get simple things to practise at home.
  5. Progress reviewsWe review what's working and adjust the plan.

Every person is different, so we don't promise fixed results or timelines. We start with an assessment, set realistic goals with you and review progress regularly.

Frequently asked questions

Do you assess for dyslexia?

We assess your child's learning strengths and difficulties and explain what we find. If a formal diagnosis is needed, we'll guide you on next steps.

Can you coordinate with my child's school?

With your permission, we can share strategies that may help at school.

How often are sessions?

We recommend a schedule after the assessment, based on your child's needs.
