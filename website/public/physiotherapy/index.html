Paediatric Physiotherapy in Sector 137, Noida

Helping little bodies move with more strength and confidence, with Dr. Samriddhi, Physiotherapist and certified Occupational Therapist.

Book an assessment Call +91 88002 30240

Who it can help

  • Babies and children reaching motor milestones (sitting, crawling, walking) later than expected
  • Children with low muscle tone or weakness
  • Children who find balance, running, jumping or stairs difficult
  • Children with cerebral palsy or other conditions that affect movement

What we work on

  • Gross motor milestones: sitting, crawling, standing and walking
  • Strength and posture
  • Balance and coordination
  • Movement through play, with exercises to continue at home

Physiotherapy for children

Our physiotherapy service is for children. Sessions are play-based, and parents learn simple exercises to continue at home.

How it works at Vocalzz

  1. AssessmentWe meet you, listen to your concerns and understand strengths and needs.
  2. A plan made togetherClear goals, shared with you, and coordinated with other therapies if needed.
  3. Regular sessionsOne-on-one, in a group or online, depending on what suits best.
  4. Home practiceAfter every session you get simple things to practise at home.
  5. Progress reviewsWe review what's working and adjust the plan.

Every person is different, so we don't promise fixed results or timelines. We start with an assessment, set realistic goals with you and review progress regularly.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer physiotherapy for adults?

Our physiotherapy service focuses on children. Message us and we'll guide you.

What happens in the first session?

The physiotherapist observes how your child moves and plays, talks with you about your concerns, and suggests a plan.
