About us

About Vocalzz

Vocalzz is a multi-therapy clinic in Sector 137, Noida for children, adults and seniors. Our promise is simple: Better Communication, Better Life.

Why families choose Vocalzz

  • Every therapy under one roof: speech, occupational and behavioural therapy, special education, audiology and paediatric physiotherapy.
  • One team, one plan: our therapists share notes and agree goals together, so nothing pulls in different directions.
  • Parents are part of therapy: after every session you get simple activities to practise at home.
  • Honest guidance: every person is different, so we set realistic goals and review progress with you, without promising fixed results or timelines.
  • Flexible: one-on-one, group and online sessions, open every day from 10 am to 9 pm.
