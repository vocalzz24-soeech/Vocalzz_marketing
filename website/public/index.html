How we work

A clear plan, and you're part of it

Therapy works best when home and clinic pull in the same direction. So we keep you involved at every step.

"Every child is different. We start where your child is, not where a chart says they should be."

Dr. Tanu Garg, Founder & Lead Clinician
  1. AssessmentWe meet you and your child, listen to your concerns and understand their strengths.
  2. One plan, made togetherSpeech, OT and behaviour therapists agree on goals, so nothing pulls in different directions.
  3. Sessions that feel like playWarm, structured sessions, one-on-one, in a group or online.
  4. Home practice after every sessionYou leave with simple things to try at home, so progress continues between visits.
  5. Regular progress check-insWe review what's working and adjust the plan as your child grows.
A quick guide for parents

Speech milestones, roughly by age

Children develop at their own pace, so use this as a gentle guide, not a test.

12 months

Babbles, turns to their name, uses gestures like waving or pointing, and may say a first word.

18 months

Says several single words and follows simple directions like "give me the ball".

2 years

Uses many words and starts putting two together, like "more milk" or "papa gaya".

3 years

Talks in short sentences; family understands most of what they say.

4 years

Tells simple stories, asks lots of "why" questions, and most people understand them.

If your child seems behind on these, has stopped using words they used to say, or doesn't respond to their name, an assessment can help you understand why and what may help.

Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

How do I book the first visit?

Send us a message on WhatsApp at +91 88002 30240 with your child's age and what you've noticed. We'll suggest a time for an assessment that suits you.

What happens in the first assessment?

We talk with you, play and interact with your child, and look at their communication, movement and behaviour. Then we explain what we observed and suggest a plan, in simple words.

How long will my child need therapy?

Every child is different, so we don't promise fixed timelines. After the assessment we'll share realistic goals and review progress with you regularly.

How long is a session?

Most sessions are around 45 minutes. We'll recommend how often to come based on your child's plan.

Do you work with adults and seniors too?

Yes. We help teens and adults who stammer, adults with voice problems, and seniors with speech difficulties after a stroke or with hearing loss.

Do you offer online therapy?

Yes, online sessions are available for families anywhere in India. Message us to check whether online therapy suits your child's needs.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any previous reports, hearing tests or school notes are helpful, but not required. Bring a favourite toy or snack if it helps your child feel at ease.

What are your fees?

Fees depend on the type of therapy and the plan. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share the details.
