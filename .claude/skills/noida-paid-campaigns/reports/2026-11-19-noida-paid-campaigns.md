# Noida Paid Campaigns Report: 2026-11-19

**Business type tracked:** Multi-therapy clinic (speech, OT, behavioural/ABA, special education, audiology, paediatric physio)
**Geography:** ~10 km of Sector 137 Noida (Expressway, Greater Noida, Greater Noida West), plus big Delhi NCR chains
**Platforms checked:** Web search (directories, press, competitor pages), our own Google Ads history (old account 164-628-8199).
**Not readable from here:** Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center and competitor websites are blocked in this
environment, so **no live competitor ads were seen and none are reported**. Use the links in "Check in your browser"
(5 minutes) to see them yourself. Spend figures for competitors are not public on any platform.

## Summary
- **The real threat is two funded chains, not the small clinics.** *Sunshine by LISSUN* raised about ₹48 crore (Aug 2026),
  runs about 25 centres heading to 50, and has Noida centres in **Sector 141 (about 2 km from you)** and Sector 121.
  *Continua Kids* (36+ centres) has Noida Sector 19, Sector 122 and Noida Extension. Both build pages around
  "speech therapy in Noida" and "child development centre in Noida", so they will bid on the same Google terms.
- **Small local rivals mostly don't advertise heavily:** The Learning Hub (same Sector 137), Centre for Autism (lists
  Sector 137), MAX@Home and Listening Ears (near Sector 128), Holding Hope and Sonakshi (Greater Noida), Brain Light and
  My Therapist (Greater Noida West). Parents already search for some of them by name (seen in our old search terms).
- **Our own data says Google search is cheap here.** The old speech campaign got clicks at ₹13–17 with a 13% click rate,
  and people searched "speech therapy in greater noida" most. Autism/ABA/OT terms will cost more (chains bid there).
- **Recommended budget:** ₹6k test in December → ₹10k in Jan–Feb → ₹12k in March → ₹15k from April, *only if* each step
  hits the targets below. Split about 60% Google search, 40% Meta Click-to-WhatsApp.
- **Expected (middle case):** about **30–35 WhatsApp/call leads and about 5 new patients a month at ₹10k**, rising to about
  **50 leads and 8 new patients at ₹15k**. Range across cases: 2–15 new patients a month at ₹10k.

## Patterns
- **Offers:** chains lead with "#1 / best centre", multi-therapy under one roof and assessments. Pinnacle publicly says
  ads must avoid "cure/normal/guaranteed", which is the same line Vocalzz follows.
- **Gaps nobody covers:** adult stammering and voice; post-stroke speech for elderly parents (reach adult children on
  Facebook); Hinglish parent-worry hooks; **Expressway sectors 137–150** (no other centre found in 143–150 besides LISSUN 141).
- **Most competition:** autism/ABA terms and "child development centre", where funded chains outbid small clinics.

## Budget and 6-month forecast (Dec 2026 – May 2027)
Model: `marketing/ads-forecast/forecast.py`. Spend split 60% Google search / 40% Meta Click-to-WhatsApp.
Season: Dec dip (holidays), Feb–Mar up (school-readiness, admissions), May up (summer intensive therapy).

| Month | Spend | Leads: low / **base** / high | New patients: low / **base** / high | Cost per lead (base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 2026 | ₹6,000 | 10 / **15** / 29 | 1 / **2** / 7 | ₹400 |
| Jan 2027 | ₹10,000 | 21 / **31** / 59 | 2–3 / **5** / 15 | ₹327 |
| Feb 2027 | ₹10,000 | 24 / **35** / 67 | 3 / **5** / 17 | ₹289 |
| Mar 2027 | ₹12,000 | 30 / **44** / 85 | 4 / **7** / 21 | ₹273 |
| Apr 2027 | ₹15,000 | 35 / **51** / 99 | 4 / **8** / 24 | ₹295 |
| May 2027 | ₹15,000 | 37 / **54** / 105 | 4–5 / **8–9** / 26 | ₹277 |
| **6 months** | **₹68,000** | 158 / **229** / 444 | 19 / **36** / 110 | ₹297 |

"Lead" = a WhatsApp chat or call from a real parent. Base case assumes ₹26 per Google click, 6% of clicks become a lead,
₹90 per Meta WhatsApp chat with 35% genuine, 35% of leads book an assessment, 45% of assessments start therapy.
These are assumptions; replace them with real numbers after Month 1. **Don't scale up a month unless:** cost per
lead under ₹450 and at least 3 new patients from ads in the previous month.

**Is it worth it?** At base case, one new patient costs about ₹1,800–2,500 in ads. Competitor fees are ₹650–1,500 per
session, so a child attending 2 sessions a week pays back the ad cost in the first 1–2 weeks. Check against Vocalzz's own fee.

**Ceiling:** local search demand is limited (the old campaign saw only about 1,500 impressions in 17 days for speech
terms). Above roughly ₹15–20k a month, extra money mostly buys costlier, lower-intent clicks. Grow through more
services (OT, stammering, hearing), not just more budget on the same words.

## Recommendations for Vocalzz
1. **Track the right conversions first** (new Google Ads account owned by Vocalzz): WhatsApp clicks and calls, not
   "directions". Add the "How did you hear about us" source to the booking app so ads are judged by patients, not clicks.
2. **Google search, exact/phrase match only, no display/partners:** start with speech therapy + Greater Noida/Noida/
   "near me" (proven), add stammering/adult speech and OT next. Negatives: competitor names, Delhi/Gurgaon/Faridabad,
   AIIMS/Safdarjung/govt, jobs/course/salary. Radius ~10–12 km around Sector 137 including Greater Noida.
3. **Meta Click-to-WhatsApp:** one parent worry per ad, Hinglish, Dr. Tanu on camera (e.g. "2 saal ka bachha abhi
   bhi nahi bolta? Ye 5 signs dekhiye"). No cure, timeline or "best/#1" claims (also against Meta health ad policy).
4. **Avoid bidding hard on "autism centre/ABA"** early: the funded chains own those terms. Win on speech, stammering
   and the Expressway location ("Sector 137, Logix Blossom County: 5 minutes from your society").
5. **Google Business Profile stays the cheapest lead source** (about 11 calls + 85 direction requests a month for free).
   Keep posting weekly, ask every family for a review, and fix the duplicate listings.
